Welcome to my homepage!

I am an Assistant Professor of Instruction in the [Department of Statistics and Data Science](https://statistics.northwestern.edu/) at [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/). 
Before joining Northwestern, I was a Data Scientist at [Pandora Bio](https://pandorabio.com/).  I received my PhD in Mathematics from [The Ohio State University](https://math.osu.edu/) in 2024 where I was advised by [Prof. Matthew Kahle](https://www.matthewkahle.org/). I also got a minor in Computer Science and a master degree in Mathematics from the Ohio State University. Prior to that I completed my BS–MS in Mathematics at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune](https://www.iiserpune.ac.in/), in 2018, where my master’s thesis in analytic number theory was advised by [Prof. Kaneenika Sinha](https://sites.iiserpune.ac.in/~kaneenika/).

My research spans combinatorics, graph theory, and machine learning. I’m also a passionate educator, recognized for inclusive teaching and mentorship. I’m deeply interested in using data science to improve education and student mental health.
